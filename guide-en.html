Your first recording.

Run the installer and follow the setup wizard, then open CatEye from your desktop or Start menu. Uninstall through Windows Installed apps; your recordings are kept. If you download a portable ZIP, extract everything and keep NAudio.dll and the tools folder.

Choose the quality that fits.

MP4

High quality

Keeps original pixel dimensions with high-quality lossy compression. Usually smaller files for everyday recording, demos and sharing.

MKV

Lossless RGB

Preserves RGB pixels for fine text, graphics and editing. Larger files; use VLC or a compatible editor.

Lossless refers to video pixels; audio uses AAC compression. File size depends on content, motion, duration and frame rate. There is no fixed compression ratio.

Stay in the flow. Stay in control.

Pause / resumeCtrl + Shift + F9
Stop and saveCtrl + Shift + F10

The full floating toolbar requires Windows 10 2004 or later with desktop composition. Older systems use tray controls and hotkeys.

Remote-desktop apps may hide the capture-excluded toolbar. Use the shortcuts above in that case.

A few common questions.

Really free? Really no watermark?

Yes. Every feature is free, recordings have no watermark, and no account or subscription is required.

Does it record audio?

Record computer audio, your microphone, both together, or mute. Audio and video pause together. Choose the source that fits your class, meeting or tutorial.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Targets 64-bit Windows 7 SP1 / 8 / 8.1 / 10 / 11 with .NET Framework 4.5.2 or later. Regression testing was completed on Windows 10 x64; other targets still need device validation.

Does recording need an internet connection?

Recording works offline. Videos go directly to the local folder you choose. Update checks and downloads require internet access.

Why can the toolbar disappear in a remote session?

Some remote-desktop apps also hide the capture-excluded toolbar. Use Ctrl + Shift + F9 to pause or resume and Ctrl + Shift + F10 to stop and save.

The app includes Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. Switch languages at the top right of the app.
