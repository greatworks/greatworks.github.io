A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity,
before you start.

A few practical answers about recording with CatEye.

Really free? Really no watermark?

Yes. Every feature is free, recordings have no watermark, and no account or subscription is required.

Does it record audio?

Record computer audio, your microphone, both together, or mute. Audio and video pause together. Choose the source that fits your class, meeting or tutorial.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Targets 64-bit Windows 7 SP1 / 8 / 8.1 / 10 / 11 with .NET Framework 4.5.2 or later. Regression testing was completed on Windows 10 x64; other targets still need device validation.

Does recording need an internet connection?

Recording works offline. Videos go directly to the local folder you choose. Update checks and downloads require internet access.

Why can the toolbar disappear in a remote session?

Some remote-desktop apps also hide the capture-excluded toolbar. Use Ctrl + Shift + F9 to pause or resume and Ctrl + Shift + F10 to stop and save.
